Inkling

Draw a line and it sings. Lift the pen and it comes alive.

Draw
Height sets the pitch, pressure sets the volume.
Tilt
Lean the pen for a brighter, sharper tone.
Hover
Creatures swim toward your pen and wake up. Hold the pen button while hovering to scare them off.
Button
Hold the pen button while drawing for a drum creature. Low is kick, middle is snare, high is hat.
Shape
Tight curls play chords. Closed loops become drones. Long straight lines run low, as bass. Jagged turns break into an arpeggio. Spirals sweep the filter open or shut. Draw small for bells, big for pads. Creatures that touch answer each other, and three huddled close share one chord.
Key
Pick a scale and key up top. Every creature stays in tune with it, and follows if you change it.
